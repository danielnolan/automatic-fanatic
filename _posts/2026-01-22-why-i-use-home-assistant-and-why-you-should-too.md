Not that long ago I had a house full of Google and Nest devices. As my privacy concerns with big tech started to grow, I went looking for a better way — and found Home Assistant.

## What's Covered

In this video I cover why I made the switch to Home Assistant, what it is, and why I think it's the best platform for running a smart home. I get into the privacy benefits of local control, how I've replaced cloud-connected devices with locally controlled ones using Zigbee and Z-Wave, and how my setup has evolved over time.

I also cover how to get started, including hardware options for running Home Assistant whether you're a beginner or a bit more technical.
